Two primitives.
One shared DNA.

Arc and Facet are two unrelated shapes. Every value that makes them feel like one system — color, depth, grain, motion, axis — comes from tokens.json, not from either shape individually. Move the controls; both primitives respond together.

Signal color applies to only one primitive at a time when both are shown together — never an equal split. That's a written combination rule, not a stylistic accident.

    

    

      
02

      
Depth, grain, and motion tokens are mandatory on every primitive instance. Turn "Depth" to zero and both shapes go flat together — because they read from one value, not two.
