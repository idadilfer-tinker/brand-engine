# Stage 1 -- Discovery

Read `../_shared/protocol.md` first.

## What this is for

Before anything gets designed, this stage answers three questions: what
equity already exists and is worth keeping, who else occupies this
category and how, and what formats the system actually has to survive in.
Skipping this stage is the single most common reason an engine breaks later
-- primitives get chosen against the brief in front of the team, not against
the format that shows up eight months later.

## Triage

First response should establish, in one to two questions: is there an
existing brand at all, and if so, has the client already decided to evolve
it (this stage) vs. is still deciding whether to (route to
`brand-engine-audit` instead -- don't do audit's job here).

## Diagnosis

Work through these, one question at a time, not as a form:

- What visual or conceptual elements already carry meaning for this brand,
  if any? A shape, a color, a gesture, a phrase.
- Who are the closest competitors, and what do their systems do that this
  one should deliberately not repeat?
- What formats does the brand need to work in *today* -- and what's a
  format it doesn't need today but plausibly will in 12 months? (This
  second half is the one clients usually forget to mention and the one
  that breaks two-primitive systems the hardest.)
- Is there a stated budget or timeline constraint that should shape scope
  (Tier 1 Audit vs. Tier 2 Refresh vs. Tier 3 Build, per
  `docs/service-offering.md`)?

Label anything not yet confirmed by the client as a working judgment, not a
fact -- especially "what the brand's equity means," which is often the
skill's inference, not something the client has ever said in those words.

## Closure conditions

Only produce the Discovery Brief once:

- It's known whether prior equity exists and, if so, what's worth keeping.
- At least two real competitors have been named and characterized.
- A format inventory exists covering both current and 12-month-plausible
  needs.
- It's clear which engagement tier (Audit / Refresh / Build / Retainer)
  this is heading toward.

## Formal artifact: Discovery Brief

Use the shared output shell. Stage-specific content:

- **Current judgment**: from-zero build, or evolve-existing-equity refresh.
- **Key evidence**: named equity elements, named competitors, category
  convention this brand should avoid repeating.
- **Primary thread**: the one tension most likely to shape primitive choice
  (e.g., "strong existing shape, weak existing color" or "no equity, but a
  crowded category with one obvious cliche to avoid").
- **What not to do yet**: don't pick primitives yet, don't discard existing
  equity without deeper reason, don't commit to a format list that's only
  today's brief.
- **Recommended next step**: `brand-engine-02-primitive-selection`.
- **Key conclusions for next skill**: equity to preserve or discard, format
  inventory, category convention to avoid.
